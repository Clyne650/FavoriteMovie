The Godfather

Introduction

The story follows the Corleone family, a powerful Italian-American Mafia family in New York. The aging patriarch, Vito Corleone, runs the family's criminal empire, while his son Michael initially wants nothing to do with the family business. After a violent conflict puts the family in danger, Michael gets pulled deeper into the Mafia world. The movie then follows Michael's transformation from someone who wants a legitimate life into someone who increasingly takes control of the family. A big theme is the contrast between “family man” and “crime boss”—and how protecting your family can become an excuse for increasingly ruthless actions.

Plot

The Godfather follows Michael Corleone, the son of Mafia boss Vito Corleone. After his father is attacked, Michael gets involved in the family's criminal business. He eventually takes control of the Corleone family, becoming a powerful Mafia boss despite originally wanting a normal life.

Reviews

The Godfather (1972) is very highly acclaimed. Critics especially praise its acting, storytelling, cinematography, and character development.

  • Rotten Tomatoes: 97% critics, 98% audience.
  • Metacritic: 100/100 from 16 critic reviews.
